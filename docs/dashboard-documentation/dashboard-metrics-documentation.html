👥 Total Footfall 335 customers

Definition

Total Footfall represents the total number of unique customers who visited the T Nagar branch during the specified 7-day period. This includes all customers who entered the branch premises, regardless of the services they used or the duration of their visit.

Explanation

This metric measures the customer traffic volume and is a primary indicator of branch popularity and accessibility. It tracks both peak and off-peak periods, helping identify patterns in customer behavior. The count includes all entry types: walk-ins, scheduled appointments, and repeat visits.

What Can a Manager Do With This Data

  • Staffing Optimization: Adjust staff schedules based on customer volume patterns
  • Resource Planning: Ensure adequate counter availability during high-traffic periods
  • Marketing Strategy: Identify low-traffic days for promotional activities
  • Branch Performance: Compare against other branches and set improvement targets
  • Capacity Management: Plan for expansion or optimize space utilization

Calculation Formula

Database Query:
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT customer_entry_id) as total_footfall FROM customer_entries WHERE branch_id = 2 -- T Nagar Branch AND entry_date BETWEEN DATE_SUB(CURDATE(), INTERVAL 7 DAY) AND CURDATE() AND status = 'COMPLETED'
Business Logic:
Total Footfall = Sum of all unique customer entries per day over 7 days Daily Average = Total Footfall ÷ 7 days Growth Rate = ((Current Period - Previous Period) ÷ Previous Period) × 100

Definition

Peak Hour Traffic represents the maximum number of customers served in any single hour during the 7-day period. This metric identifies the busiest hour of operation when customer demand is at its highest, typically occurring between 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM.

Explanation

This metric helps identify operational pressure points and customer behavior patterns. Peak hours usually coincide with lunch breaks, salary days, or specific banking needs. Understanding peak hours is crucial for maintaining service quality during high-demand periods.

What Can a Manager Do With This Data

  • Staff Scheduling: Deploy maximum staff during peak hours
  • Queue Management: Implement advanced queue systems during busy periods
  • Service Optimization: Streamline processes to handle higher volumes
  • Customer Communication: Inform customers about busy hours for better planning
  • Capacity Planning: Ensure sufficient counters and infrastructure
  • Performance Monitoring: Track service levels during peak times

Calculation Formula

Database Query:
SELECT MAX(hourly_count) as peak_hour_traffic FROM ( SELECT HOUR(entry_time) as hour_slot, COUNT(*) as hourly_count FROM customer_entries WHERE branch_id = 2 AND entry_date BETWEEN DATE_SUB(CURDATE(), INTERVAL 7 DAY) AND CURDATE() GROUP BY DATE(entry_date), HOUR(entry_time) ) hourly_stats
Business Logic:
Peak Hour Traffic = MAX(customers_per_hour) across all hours in 7 days Utilization Rate = (Peak Hour Traffic ÷ Branch Capacity) × 100 Efficiency Index = Peak Hour Traffic ÷ Average Hourly Traffic

Definition

Service Efficiency measures how effectively the branch processes customer requests relative to optimal performance standards. It combines average wait time, transaction speed, and staff utilization to provide a comprehensive efficiency score. Higher percentages indicate better operational performance.

Explanation

This composite metric evaluates operational effectiveness across multiple dimensions. It considers how quickly customers are served, how efficiently staff processes transactions, and how well resources are utilized. An efficiency above 80% indicates excellent operational performance.

Component Breakdown

Service Efficiency Components:
1. Average Wait Time: 3.2 minutes (Target: ≤5 minutes) 2. Transaction Speed: 94% (Transactions completed within target time) 3. Staff Utilization: 87% (Staff productivity during operational hours) Efficiency Score = (Wait Time Score × 0.4) + (Transaction Speed × 0.3) + (Staff Utilization × 0.3)

What Can a Manager Do With This Data

  • Process Improvement: Identify bottlenecks and streamline operations
  • Staff Management: Optimize staff allocation and productivity
  • Technology Utilization: Leverage digital tools to improve efficiency
  • Training Programs: Focus on areas with lower efficiency scores
  • Performance Targets: Set realistic efficiency goals for the team
  • Resource Optimization: Balance human and technical resources effectively

Calculation Formula

Database Query:
SELECT ROUND(AVG(wait_time_minutes), 2) as avg_wait_time, ROUND( (COUNT(CASE WHEN service_time_minutes <= target_time THEN 1 END) * 100.0 / COUNT(*)) , 2) as transaction_speed_pct, ROUND( (SUM(service_time_minutes) / (staff_count * operational_hours * 60)) * 100 , 2) as staff_utilization_pct FROM customer_entries WHERE branch_id = 2 AND entry_date BETWEEN DATE_SUB(CURDATE(), INTERVAL 7 DAY) AND CURDATE()
Business Logic:
Wait Time Score = MAX(0, (Target_Time - Actual_Time) / Target_Time × 100) Transaction Speed = (On-time Completions ÷ Total Transactions) × 100 Staff Utilization = (Active Service Time ÷ Total Available Time) × 100 Service Efficiency = (Wait Time Score × 0.4) + (Transaction Speed × 0.3) + (Staff Utilization × 0.3)
